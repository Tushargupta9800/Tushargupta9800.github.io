2048++ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2048++ (“the app”) handles information. The app is developed by Algoristy.

Information we collect

2048++ does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photographs, payment information, or other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, scores, settings, achievements, theme preferences, and gyroscope preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not uploaded to our servers.

Gyroscope and sensor data

If you enable gyroscope controls, the app reads accelerometer data to detect the direction in which you tilt your device. Sensor readings are processed temporarily on the device, are not stored, and are not transmitted to us or third parties. You can disable gyroscope controls at any time.

Sharing results

If you choose to share a game result, the app opens your device’s native sharing interface. The information you choose to share is handled by the selected app or service according to its own privacy policy. 2048++ does not receive a copy of your shared content.

Advertising and analytics

The current version does not display advertisements and does not include third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services. If these features are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not require an account and does not collect personal information, no personal profile is created.

Data security and deletion

Because game data is stored locally, you can delete it by using the app’s reset-data option or by uninstalling the app. We do not maintain a copy of this local data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at algoristy@gmail.com.
