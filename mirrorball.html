Mirror Ball Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Mirror Ball is an offline-first game. The current version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information we collect

Mirror Ball does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photographs, payment information, or other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress and preferences locally on your device so it can provide its features. This may include scores, achievements, completed runs, game settings, ball speed, sound and music preferences, haptics preferences, tutorial status, and cosmetic selections. This information is not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

Mirror Ball uses optional haptic feedback when enabled in Settings. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files. Gameplay and progress are designed to work without an account or internet connection.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

The current version does not display advertisements and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or online leaderboards. Ads are not integrated at this time. If these practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated before the relevant feature is enabled.

Children’s privacy

Mirror Ball does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. Because the app does not require an account and does not collect personal information, no personal profile is created.

Data security and deletion

Game data is stored in the app’s private local storage. You can delete this data by clearing the app’s data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. We do not maintain a copy of this local data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact algoristy@gmail.com.

Mirror Ball is an independent game developed by Algoristy. This policy applies only to the Mirror Ball application and not to Google Play or other services.
