Twin Lanes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Applies to Twin Lanes for Android (package com.twin.lanes)

Twin Lanes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline, and everything it remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Twin Lanes has no accounts, no sign-in, no contact forms, and no server of its own. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so game data cannot be sent from the app.

Information stored on your device

To make the game work the way you expect, Twin Lanes saves a small amount of data in its private app storage using the Android preferences mechanism:

  • Your best score.
  • Your chosen speed (Relaxed, Classic, or Turbo).
  • Your sound, haptics, and light/dark theme settings.
  • Whether you have already seen the "How to play" card.

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall Twin Lanes or clear the app's data from Android settings.

Permissions

Twin Lanes declares only the normal Android VIBRATE permission, which is granted automatically and used solely for optional haptic feedback when you switch lanes. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings. The app requests no runtime permissions and no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or photos.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Twin Lanes contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not use cookies, tracking identifiers, or advertising IDs, and we do not share or sell data to anyone.

Children's privacy

Twin Lanes is suitable for players of all ages and is designed not to collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data and has no communication features, no personal information from a child can be gathered through it. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by other means, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

All game data stays within the app's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. We hold no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to retain, secure, or delete on a server. Uninstalling the app removes the stored data.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Twin Lanes changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version of this policy will always be available at the same public URL.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in Twin Lanes can be sent to algoristy@gmail.com.

Twin Lanes is an independent game. This policy covers the Twin Lanes Android application only and does not apply to the Google Play Store or any other service.
